About IF Korea

Your bridge into
the Korean market.

IF Korea is Impact Foundation's official K-Scouter program, authorized by Korea's Ministry of SMEs and Startups. We find, guide, and connect global founders ready to establish a real presence in Korea.

Since 2018, we've built active networks across Vietnam, Hong Kong, Japan, and beyond — giving us a genuine on-the-ground view of what it takes to land successfully in Asia's most connected innovation market.

We specialize in healthcare and tech, but we're open to any sector where we see genuine potential. If your startup is ready for Korea, we want to hear from you.
